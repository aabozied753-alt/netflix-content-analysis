# Netflix Content Analysis

An Excel dashboard analyzing 8,807 Netflix titles (movies and TV shows) by type, genre, release year, country and content rating.

## Key findings
- 70% movies and 30% TV shows
- Dramas and Comedies are the top genres
- Release volume peaked around 2018, with a sharp rise after 2014
- The United States leads with 3,211 titles, followed by India with 1,008
- TV-MA (36%) and TV-14 (25%) dominate the ratings
- 831 titles have an unknown country, a data quality gap
